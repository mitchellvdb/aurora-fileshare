Receiving

    Your browser will hold each file in memory before saving it, so very large files may fail. Streaming straight to disk needs an HTTPS connection.

    Encrypted end to end by DTLS. Nothing passes through our servers.

    Where do these files come from?

    01

    Directly from the sender's browser to yours — there is no copy on a server.

    02

    Our server only introduced the two browsers. It never sees the bytes.

    03

    If the sender closes their tab, the share ends. Their device is the source.
