Terms of use.

Short, because the service is small: it introduces two browsers to each other and then gets out of the way.

What the service does

Aurora FileShare helps one browser send files directly to another. The files travel over a direct, encrypted connection between the two browsers; they are never uploaded to, stored on or relayed through our server. What the server does see, and for how long, is described in the FAQ.

What you may not use it for

Do not use it to send anything that is illegal where you or the recipient are. That includes, among other things, child sexual abuse material, material that infringes someone else's copyright, malware sent to people who did not ask for it, and anything used to threaten or harass someone.

You are responsible for what you send. The recipient decides whether to accept it.

What we do, and what we cannot do

We do not see the contents of files and we do not monitor what is sent. We can end a share while it is live, which also stops a transfer in progress, and we do that when a share is reported to us or when we are ordered to. We comply with lawful orders from the competent authorities, to the extent that we hold anything: nothing is written to disk, so once a share has ended there is nothing left to hand over.

Reporting misuse

Send the share link and a short description of the problem to . If the share is still live we close it. A link also stops working on its own as soon as the sender closes their tab.

The same address is the point of contact for authorities and for users under the EU Digital Services Act. You can write to us in English or Dutch.

No guarantees

The service is free and provided as it is. Transfers can fail, most often because the two browsers cannot reach each other. As far as the law allows, we are not liable for failed or incomplete transfers or for what someone else sends you.
