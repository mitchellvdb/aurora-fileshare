      No size limit

      Why there is no size limit

      Most transfer services cap what you can send because every file you upload occupies a disk they pay for, for as long as it sits there. The ceiling on a free tier is where that cost stops being worth absorbing.

      Nothing is uploaded here, so that cost never arises. Your browser and the recipient's connect directly and the bytes cross the network once, from your machine to theirs. Our server only passes along the details the two browsers need in order to find each other — a few kilobytes of connection data — and then has no further part in it.

      The trade-off is real and worth knowing before you start: both people have to be online at the same time, and the sending tab has to stay open, because your device is the source. If you need a file to sit and wait for someone, a service that stores it is the better tool.
